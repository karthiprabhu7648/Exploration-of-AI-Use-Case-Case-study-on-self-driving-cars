# Exploration of AI Use Case – Case Study on Self-Driving Cars

## AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

## THEORY

Self-driving cars are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI), sensors, cameras, and advanced computer systems to drive with minimal or no human intervention. AI helps these vehicles identify roads, detect obstacles, recognize traffic signals, and respond to changing traffic conditions.

The major AI technologies used in self-driving cars are:

1. Computer Vision: Uses cameras to identify road signs, traffic lights, pedestrians, vehicles, and lane markings.
2. Machine Learning: Helps the vehicle learn from driving data and improve its ability to recognize objects and road conditions.
3. Sensor Fusion: Combines information from cameras, radar, and LiDAR to understand the vehicle's surroundings more accurately.
4. Path Planning: Determines the safest and most suitable route for the vehicle to follow.
5. Decision Making: Helps the car decide when to brake, accelerate, change lanes, or stop based on traffic conditions.
6. Navigation: Uses GPS and digital maps to determine the vehicle's location and reach its destination.

Working Principle:

First, the sensors and cameras collect information about the surroundings. AI systems process this information to detect obstacles, identify traffic signals, and understand road conditions. The decision-making system then selects suitable actions, and the vehicle control system operates the steering, brakes, and accelerator accordingly.

Example: Companies such as Tesla and Waymo develop vehicles that use AI-based technologies to assist with or perform driving tasks. However, their capabilities and the level of human supervision required differ.

AI improves driving assistance, traffic management, and object detection. Nevertheless, self-driving technology still faces challenges such as unpredictable road conditions, bad weather, and unexpected human behavior. Therefore, appropriate testing, safety systems, and human supervision where required are essential.

## RESULT

Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied. The study explains how computer vision, machine learning, sensor fusion, navigation, and decision-making systems help vehicles understand their surroundings and perform driving tasks. AI has the potential to improve road safety and driving efficiency, although reliable operation under all road conditions remains a challenge.
